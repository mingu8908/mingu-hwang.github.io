01 About Me

Hi, I am Mingu 👋

I am a CS Student at Stony Brook University who enjoys working with AI out of research and into working systems.

At SM Solutions, I built a retrieval pipeline for an internal LLM that converts technical PDFs into structured Markdown, chunks them by heading hierarchy, and combines keyword search with semantic vector search in ChromaDB.

At MindSyn, I ported a Spike-Driven Transformer from PyTorch to dependency-free C and ran it on a KEIT neuromorphic chip, matching the original model's accuracy on MNIST.

Beyond engineering, I love teaching. I led a team of 5+ tutors who designed Java and Python courses for elementary through high school students.

I work mainly in Python, C, Java, and SQL, and I am currently seeking opportunities to apply my skills in AI/ML or Software Engineering.
